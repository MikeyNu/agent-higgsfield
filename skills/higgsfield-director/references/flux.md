# FLUX
Checked 2026-10-18. Separate FLUX.2, FLUX 3 and Kontext. Resolve the current version and schema before transferring guidance.

## FLUX.2 prompting
Put the important subject and action first, followed by essential style/context and secondary detail. Describe the desired appearance positively. FLUX.2 has no negative-prompt control.

Use natural language for straightforward compositions. Use JSON only when named subjects, positions and relationships make revision clearer. Structure is an organization tool, not a guarantee of fidelity. Tie color codes to specific objects and identify the contribution of each reference.

## Surface checks
The current Higgsfield FLUX.2 schema exposes variant pro/flex/max and resolution. Provider documentation discusses guidance, steps and prompt_upsampling, but these are not necessarily available through Higgsfield. Never invent missing fields.

Treat Kontext as a distinct editing route. Start with a localized change instruction and protected properties, then inspect for unintended changes. Detailed current Kontext guidance was not successfully retrieved during this research; fetch its official guide before relying on specialized behavior.

## Photographic direction and FLUX 3

Use concrete source/direction/quality of light, framing and surface response. A named camera or film stock is an appearance cue, not optical calibration. Current FLUX 3 guidance also uses positive descriptions and has no negative-prompt field. Its provider warns that higher-resolution regeneration can change composition; preserve an accepted master and evaluate a suitable upscale/edit instead. Do not transfer provider layout or camera fields into Higgsfield without schema support.

## Original example
"Three matte-black sculptural fins stand in a bright gallery, each separated by a visible gap. The nearest fin fills the right foreground; the other two recede toward a sunlit opening. White plaster, soft contact shadows, a 35 mm architectural perspective. The left wall remains an uninterrupted pale surface."

For an edit: "Change only the nearest fin's material to brushed aluminium. Preserve its silhouette, position, scale and the camera framing."

## Failure gates
Inspect shape count, silhouette, materials and reference fidelity. Color codes describe intent; verify sampled colors under the expected lighting. For exact logos or typography, use approved deterministic artwork. Do not reuse an earlier version's parameter names or negative syntax.
