# Seedance

Checked 2026-10-19. Distinguish 2.0, 2.5, generation, edit and extension. Inspect the selected Higgsfield route.

## Compile the request

For **T2V**, specify subject/action, environment/light, camera and style. For **I2V**, retain the approved image's design and describe what changes. For **reference generation**, assign each attachment one purpose: appearance, environment, camera/motion or audio. Use actual attachment handles, not invented tags.

For complex 2.5 work, use short labeled sections: reference roles, global look, timed beats, continuity, audio. Remove irrelevant sections. A source storyboard describes finished shots; explicitly avoid rendering its panels or annotations as footage.

Higgsfield distinguishes references within a clip from saved Elements across separate jobs [1]. ByteDance documents stronger reference interpretation, clay-render workflows and targeted time-based edits in 2.5 [2]. These guide route selection; they do not guarantee geometry or exact timing.

For photographic performance, assign a concrete action, attention target and settling state. Inspect hand/object contacts and identity/material persistence after occlusion. ByteDance explicitly acknowledges complex-motion physics and multi-subject interaction limits. Use supported blockout/performance references when needed, but do not remove an essential action just to make the test easier.

## Original reference example

> References: image A establishes the white sculpture, materials and lighting. Video B supplies only the forward camera path.\
> Look: preserve image A's monochrome palette.\
> Action: one uninterrupted move through the open passage, ending in a wide view of the same sculpture. The sculpture remains rigid and stationary.\
> Continuity: maintain the same three forms, scale and light direction.\
> Audio: silent.

Replace A/B with verified handles. Put supported duration, ratio and audio settings in parameters. Narrative timestamps are soft requests; use actual keyframes or editing when exact timing matters.

## Diagnose before revising

Camera ignored: replace vague language with one directional move. Identity changes: improve reference clarity or use an Element. Spatial passage collapses: supply a simple camera blockout or render geometry deterministically. Local defect: inspect supported editing before replacing the complete clip.

**Documentation conflicts:** a Higgsfield blog says 50 images; provider and help specify 30 images plus ten videos and ten audio clips. Generic 4K prose also conflicts with some 2.5 schemas. The active route determines limits.
