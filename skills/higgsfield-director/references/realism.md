# Direct believable photographs and footage

Use for photographic, live-action and hybrid briefs. Treat this as an art-direction method, not a recipe for guaranteed human indistinguishability. The specific imperfection choices below are engineering and creative recommendations, not measured success rates.

## Choose the intended reality

Name the treatment: observational photography, controlled commercial photography, live-action cinema, stylized CG/animation, or a defined hybrid. Preserve the requested treatment. A pristine studio product can be realistic; a cartoon can be deliberately impossible. Judge hybrid CG against its own design while matching the photographed layer's perspective, light and contact.

Separate visual plausibility from factual authenticity. If a picture must show actual staff, premises, a manufactured product or a documented event, anchor it in authorized real references and verify those facts. A convincing invented scene cannot prove the business owns that office or employs those people. Do not describe generated material as camera-captured or remove provenance to claim it is human-made.

## Make a small director's brief

Before compiling the model prompt, resolve these decisions. Infer routine choices from the brief; ask only when a missing choice changes an essential requirement or costly step.

| Decision | What to specify |
| --- | --- |
| Moment | A concrete event and why this instant matters. Give subjects something plausible to do, notice or respond to. |
| Camera | Viewpoint, subject distance, framing, focus target and reason for movement or stillness. Choose deliberate composition, not automatic centered beauty-shot framing. |
| Light | A motivated key source, plausible fill/practicals, shadow softness, highlight treatment and exposure. Different surfaces must inhabit the same lighting setup. |
| Materials | Skin, fabric, glass, metal or paper at the correct scale, finish and condition. Preserve identity and exact product geometry. |
| Variation | Usually zero to three relevant signs of life or use. Choose their locations and causes; do not dump a list of defects into every prompt. |
| Acceptance | Observable essentials and disqualifying defects at the actual delivery size and viewing conditions. |

Camera names, focal lengths, apertures and shutter terms express appearance unless a verified tool exposes them as real controls. Keep them mutually plausible. Do not stack incompatible lenses, extreme shallow focus and an entirely sharp deep scene. Describing visible perspective and focus is preferable to invented precision. “8K,” “award-winning” and “hyperreal” do not establish capture quality.

## Choose variation that belongs

- **People:** preserve individual facial structure, natural skin variation and believable hair/fabric. Allow a relaxed weight distribution, specific eyeline and an unsynchronized response to a task. Do not force pores, freckles, wrinkles, grime or beauty retouching onto every person. Do not confuse nationality with one facial type. For South African casting, use appropriate diversity and verified context without stock stereotypes.
- **Places:** use purposeful spatial organization, credible scale and ordinary activity. An occupied desk may have a slightly displaced notebook; a newly opened showroom may be immaculate. Do not invent wear, clutter or a location's factual architecture.
- **Products:** preserve dimensions, seams, label, material and intended condition. Let controlled reflections, contact shadows and material response establish realism. New cosmetics, polished jewellery and pristine electronics do not need invented scratches or fingerprints.
- **Performance:** prefer a specific small action with a beginning, reaction and settle. Let a person finish listening before responding. Describe gaze targets and weight transfer where relevant. Do not order continual blinking, breathing, head bobbing and gesturing all at once.

Anatomical mistakes, sliding feet, floating objects and reflections that change inconsistently with the object, camera, reflected scene or light are defects, not charming imperfection. Correct reflections can change during movement; do not freeze them. Film grain, blur and aggressive grading cannot repair structural defects. Add finishing texture only if it belongs to the chosen capture aesthetic; inspect again after enhancement because it may change likeness or material detail.

## Keep video causally consistent

Define support/contact, direction of force, object permanence through occlusion, stable wardrobe/materials and a continuous light source. Request a feasible action and a motivated camera move. Tripods and controlled dollies are valid human choices; handheld shake is optional. Avoid simultaneous complex hands, liquid, speech and camera travel unless essential to the brief.

Use a clean accepted still for appearance and a supported performance/motion reference for difficult movement. In I2V, emphasize the change rather than redescribing a different person or room. If physical interactions repeatedly fail, simplify or use filmed performance, deterministic animation or compositing. More texture adjectives do not enforce physics. For audio, specify relevant room tone and timed contact sounds only when supported, then check synchronization separately.

## Compile one coherent prompt

Use the selected family's reference syntax and editing method. Keep supported controls in parameters. Prefer a compact connected description or a few labeled sections; each clause should change a visible decision. Treat this as an original example to adapt, not a universal template:

> Observational editorial photograph of two adult designers reviewing one printed layout in a contemporary Johannesburg studio. The nearer designer pauses with a pencil resting on the paper while the colleague follows the indicated detail. Eye-level three-quarter view across the table, both faces and the print in readable focus. Soft window light from the left, restrained reflected fill. Preserve individual skin texture and the natural folds at their elbows. A slightly turned sheet gives the workspace a used but orderly feel. Quiet concentration, restrained colour, clean photographic rendering.

Do not use that fictional casting prompt as proof of actual staff. For I2V of an accepted version, request only a small agreed action and camera behavior. For a local edit, name the defect and protect the accepted identity, geometry, light and framing. Apply the QA rubric before delivery; a persuasive prompt alone earns no realism pass.
